Batch watermark photos on Mac

In Image-Batch, open the Watermark tab, drop your logo, and click Watermark. A Watermarked Images folder is created with the logo on every photo.

  1. 1

    Open Image-Batch. In Step 1, leave Folder selected, then select or drag a folder of images. Edited copies go into a new folder named Watermarked Images inside that folder.

  2. 2

    In Step 2, open the Watermark tab. Select or drag a watermark image (HEIF, JPEG, PNG, TIFF, WEBP, or AVIF). Lite places it in the Bottom right. Pro and Premium can pick a Position: Top left, Top right, Center, Bottom left, or Bottom right.

  3. 3

    In Step 3, click Watermark. When Status says Watermarking Complete, you can click View Images to open the Watermarked Images folder.

Tips

  • A PNG with a transparent background makes a clean logo on top of your photos.
  • Your original photos stay untouched unless you turn on Edit original images, which is available in Pro and Premium.
