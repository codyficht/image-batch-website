Compress images to a file size on Mac

Image-Batch can compress a folder of photos on your Mac, with no upload. Lite reduces by 5%. Pro adds a percentage slider. Premium can also compress each photo to a maximum size in KB or MB.

  1. 1

    Open Image-Batch. In Step 1, leave Folder selected, then select or drag a folder of images. Edited copies go into a new folder named Reduced Images inside that folder.

  2. 2

    In Step 2, open the Reduce tab. To set a maximum size (Premium), turn on Desired file size, choose KB or MB under Unit, and enter the Max desired file size per image. Leave Allow smaller dimensions on if the photo can be made smaller to reach that file size. To reduce by a percentage instead, leave Desired file size off. Lite reduces by a fixed 5%. Pro and Premium can drag the Reduce by at least slider from 5% to 50%.

  3. 3

    In Step 3, click Reduce. When Status says Reduction Complete, you can click View Images to open the Reduced Images folder.

Tips

  • Reduce can make files smaller by compressing them, and it may also make the photo itself smaller. Always check a couple of results if the way a photo looks matters to you.
  • You can compress first, then open the Reduced Images folder in Convert or Resize if you need another change. Image-Batch runs one tool at a time.
  • Your original photos stay untouched, and copies go into a new folder, unless you turn on Edit original images (Pro and Premium), which edits the files in place.
