Batch resize images on Mac

In Image-Batch, drop a folder of photos, open the Resize tab, set a pixel size, and click Resize. The app writes the resized copies to a new folder and leaves your originals alone.

  1. 1

    Open Image-Batch. In Step 1, leave Folder selected, then select or drag a folder of images. Edited copies go into a new folder named Resized Images inside that folder.

  2. 2

    In Step 2, open the Resize tab. Under Side, choose Width (included in Lite), Height (Pro and Premium), or Largest (Premium). Type the value in the field labeled New width, New height, or New largest side, in pixels. Leave Enlarge images off if you only want to shrink photos that are already larger than that size.

  3. 3

    In Step 3, click Resize. When Status says Resizing Complete, you can click View Images to open the Resized Images folder.

Tips

  • Pro and Premium can also choose Images in Step 1 to pick individual files, and can change the Output folder name.
  • If you want every photo, portrait or landscape, to fit within one maximum size, Premium's Largest option resizes the longest side and keeps the aspect ratio.
  • Your original photos stay untouched, and copies go into a new folder, unless you turn on Edit original images (Pro and Premium), which edits the files in place.
