Guides

Short, step-by-step guides for Image-Batch, the Mac app for batch photo editing. Each guide uses the real controls in the app. Editing happens on your Mac, and your photos are not uploaded.

Batch resize images

Resize a folder of photos by width, height, or largest side.

Compress images to a file size

Shrink photos to a target size in KB or MB, or by a percentage.

Convert HEIC to JPG

Turn a folder of iPhone HEIC photos into JPEG, offline.

Convert images to WebP or AVIF

Batch convert photos to WebP, AVIF, and other formats.

Batch crop images

Crop a folder of photos to an aspect ratio or an exact size.

Batch watermark photos

Add a logo or watermark to every photo in a folder.

Batch rename photos

Give a folder of photos a shared name, a count, and a separator.

Find duplicate photos

Keep one copy of each photo and collect the unique files.
