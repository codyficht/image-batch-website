Pricing Tiers

Discover the Right Tier for Your Needs

Start free with powerful core tools. Upgrade anytime with simple one-time purchases — no subscriptions, no surprises, ever.

How to upgrade

Upgrade anytime from the Upgrade Here button at the bottom of the app.

Lite Version (FREE):

This is the basic version of Image-Batch, letting you access its foundational features.

  • Process up to 3 images at once.
  • Bulk resize (by width), reduce/compress (fixed 5%; the slider unlocks in Pro), crop, convert (JPEG only), watermark, rename, and find duplicates.

Pro Upgrade ($8.99 USD):

Step up your editing game with extended features for heavy workflows.

  • Process up to 40 images at once.
  • Enter custom output folder names.
  • Direct Edit Mode: Modify images in their original folder.
  • Extend conversion options to PNG, HEIF, and TIFF.
  • Choose custom crop and watermark positions.
  • Resize by width or height.
  • Compress and convert quality sliders.
  • Choose the rename number position and separator.
  • Direct image selection.
  • Access to the settings menu.
  • And all the core features from Lite!

Premium Upgrade ($19.99 USD) Most Popular

Experience the full potential of Image-Batch with our most comprehensive features for unlimited power.

  • Process an unlimited number of images at once. Tested with over 500 images!
  • Reduce/Compress to a specific desired file size per image.
  • Precision cropping by exact dimensions.
  • Advanced, largest side resizing.
  • Exclusive next-gen AVIF and WEBP conversion access.
  • Access to Rotate & Flip.
  • Plus, all the benefits of Pro!

If you already have Pro, the Pro to Premium upgrade is $12.99 USD. That is the step up to Premium, instead of buying Premium at the full $19.99.



Note: Previously, Image-Batch was a single one-time purchase for all features. Customers who bought that earlier edition keep lifetime Premium access, plus free updates.
