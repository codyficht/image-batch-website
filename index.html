Image-Batch

The all-in-one batch photo editor designed for Mac.

* Requires macOS 15.0 or later.

Edit Smarter: fast, intuitive batch editing for hundreds of images at once — loved by creators who value their time.

Process hundreds of images in seconds. Resize, crop, compress, convert, watermark, rename, and more — all with one powerful, intuitive app.

Compatible image formats: WEBP, AVIF, JPEG/JPG, PNG, HEIF, TIFF

Made for photographers, graphic designers, content creators, and social media managers. Under 10 MB. No internet needed to edit, and your photos stay on your Mac.

Lite is free for 3 images at a time. Pro is $8.99 for 40 at a time. Premium is $19.99 for unlimited images at a time. Both are one-time purchases. See the FAQ.

Client galleries, product shoots, social posts, iPhone photos, or any other batch you would rather not edit one image at a time.

Batch edit in 3 easy steps

1. Drop a folder

2. Pick one tool

3. Save copies

Every edition saves copies to a new folder, one tool per run. Lite selects a folder. Pro and Premium can also select individual files, and can turn on Direct Edit to change the originals.

Everything You Need for Fast, Professional Batch Editing

Resize

Resize entire batches by width, height, or largest side while perfectly maintaining aspect ratios. Professional results in seconds.Lite resizes by width. Pro adds height. Premium adds largest-side resizing.

Reduce by Percent

Shrink file sizes instantly with a simple percentage slider. Perfect for web, email, and storage optimization.Included in Lite.

Reduce by File Size

Set a maximum file size and let Image-Batch optimize every image to fit. Ideal for strict upload limits.Premium.

Convert

Convert batches to JPEG, PNG, TIFF, HEIF, AVIF, or WEBP with adjustable quality. Instant format magic in one click.Lite converts to JPEG. Pro adds PNG, HEIF, and TIFF. Premium adds AVIF and WebP.

Find Duplicates

Automatically find and remove duplicate images in any batch. Keep your library clean and organized.Included in Lite.

Watermark

Add a custom watermark image to entire batches in seconds. Protect your work with professional branding.Included in Lite. Pro and Premium add custom placement.

Rename

Rename entire batches with custom patterns and sequential numbering. Perfect organization in seconds.Included in Lite. Pro and Premium can choose the separator before the number.

Crop by Aspect Ratio

Crop batches to any aspect ratio with one click. Perfect for Instagram, YouTube, print, and more.Included in Lite. Pro and Premium add a custom crop position.

Crop by Size

Crop to exact pixel dimensions with custom positioning. Pixel-perfect control for any project.Premium.

Rotate & Flip

Rotate batches from -180° to +180° and flip horizontally or vertically. Creative control in seconds.Premium.

Comfort

Automatic light and dark mode that matches your system. Easy on the eyes, day or night.

Smart Guidance

Clear, friendly messages guide you through every step. No confusion, just smooth batch editing.
