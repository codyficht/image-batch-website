Everything You Need for Fast, Professional Batch Editing

Resize

Resize entire batches by width, height, or largest side while perfectly maintaining aspect ratios. Professional results in seconds.

Reduce by Percent

Shrink file sizes instantly with a simple percentage slider. Perfect for web, email, and storage optimization.

Reduce by File Size

Set a maximum file size and let Image-Batch optimize every image to fit. Ideal for strict upload limits.

Convert

Convert batches to JPEG, PNG, TIFF, HEIF, AVIF, or WEBP with adjustable quality. Instant format magic in one click.

Find Duplicates

Automatically find and remove duplicate images in any batch. Keep your library clean and organized.

Watermark

Add a custom watermark image to entire batches in seconds. Protect your work with professional branding.

Rename

Rename entire batches with custom patterns and sequential numbering. Perfect organization in seconds.

Crop by Aspect Ratio

Crop batches to any aspect ratio with one click. Perfect for Instagram, YouTube, print, and more.

Crop by Size

Crop to exact pixel dimensions with custom positioning. Pixel-perfect control for any project.

Rotate & Flip

Rotate batches from -180° to +180° and flip horizontally or vertically. Creative control in seconds.

Comfort

Automatic light and dark mode that matches your system. Easy on the eyes, day or night.

Smart Guidance

Clear, friendly messages guide you through every step. No confusion, just smooth batch editing.

Safety First

All edits create new files in a dedicated output folder by default. Pro and Premium can turn on direct editing when you want to change the originals.

Flexible Selection

Drag in a folder of images. That is included in Lite. Pro and Premium can also select individual image files.
